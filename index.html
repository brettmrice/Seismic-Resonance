Inspiration & Credits

  • Inspiration: Inspired by Google DeepMind's Introducing Gemini 4 Hydrogen announcement and the real-time global phenomenon sonification example shown in this YouTube video.
  • Built With: Google Antigravity was used to build this web application.
  • Data Sources: Live earthquake data from the USGS Earthquake Hazards Program GeoJSON feeds, paired with world landmass and tectonic plate boundary datasets.

What This Web App Is

Seismic Resonance transforms global tectonic activity into a tactile, spatial low-frequency soundscape (24 Hz–420 Hz, visualized on a 20 Hz–450 Hz stereo spectrum). As earthquakes occur—or replay in a continuous time-lapse stream—each tremor is rendered as an expanding seismic wave on a full-bleed world map and synthesized in real time using a custom Web Audio sub-bass engine.

Key Features & Sonification Mapping

  • Live USGS Seismic Feeds: Switch between Past Hour (All), Past 24 Hours (All), Past 24 Hours (M2.5+), and Past 7 Days (M4.5+), with automatic polling every 30 seconds.
  • Time-Lapse Stream & Live Mode: Replay recent global activity at 60×, 180×, 360×, or 900× speed, or switch to Live Only to listen strictly for newly arriving earthquakes.
  • Low-Frequency Sub-Bass Synth: Choose between Tectonic Rumble, Sub-Bass Pulse, and Abyssal Pad timbres across four harmonic scales (Deep Dorian, Phrygian Dominant, Minor Pentatonic, Just Intonation Drone), with adjustable root frequency (27.5–65.4 Hz) and an optional continuous 41 Hz Earth Drone.
  • Spatial & Depth Mapping: Depth is color-coded—Shallow (< 70 km, Magma Coral), Mid (70–300 km, Amber Gold), and Deep (≥ 300 km, Abyssal Cyan)—and mapped to pitch/envelope, while longitude pans across the stereo field into dual L/R spectrum analyzers.
  • Interactive Map: Pan and zoom across the globe, toggle tectonic plate boundaries, click any quake to audition its sound, or Shift + Click anywhere on the map to trigger a synthetic tremor.
